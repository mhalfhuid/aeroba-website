How it works

Four steps.
Then close your eyes.

  1. 1

    Earphones in

    Any earphones work: wired, Bluetooth or AirPods. The tone needs to be close, so a session only starts once they’re connected.

  2. 2

    Phone on your belly

    Lie back or recline, and rest your iPhone just below your ribs. It feels your belly rise as you breathe in and fall as you breathe out. Yes, really. It’s oddly comfortable.

  3. 3

    Follow the tone

    Each time you hear it, breathe out with it, slowly. Let the in-breath come by itself. The tone starts near your own pace and leads you down, step by gentle step.

  4. 4

    See your breath

    When the session ends, you see your breath as a line, and the tone drawn above it. Most people are surprised how far they went.

Why slow breathing

The one part of your nervous system you can steer.

You can’t tell your heart to slow down. You can slow your breath, and your body follows. Breathing around six to seven times a minute is the pace many breathing practices settle into on their own. Aeroba simply gets you there without you needing to know any of that.

Aeroba is a wellness tool, not a medical device. If you have a heart, lung or anxiety condition, talk to your doctor before starting any breathing practice.

Questions

The things people actually ask.

I’ve never meditated. Is this for me?

Especially for you. There’s nothing to learn first. If you can breathe out when you hear a sound, you can do an Aeroba session.

I tried meditation apps and gave up. Why would this be different?

Most apps ask you to manage your attention. Aeroba gives your attention one simple job, following a tone, while your breath does the real work. And it shows you afterwards that something actually changed.

Why on my belly?

Your belly moves most when you breathe slowly and deeply. Lying there, the iPhone’s motion sensors can feel every breath, so the tone can wait for you instead of running ahead.

Do I need special earphones?

No. Wired, Bluetooth or AirPods all work. A session starts once earphones are connected.

How long is a session?

About ten minutes. The app shows when a session will end before you start, so you can silence alarms first.

When can I get it?

Aeroba is in a small pilot right now and comes to the App Store soon. Until then, try the tone in your browser.

Is it a medical app?

No. Aeroba is a wellness tool for relaxation. It doesn’t diagnose or treat anything.
